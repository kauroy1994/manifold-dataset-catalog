## Original Future Factories data

The 2024 and 2025 source papers report **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** for their datasets. The catalog preserves that statement and its source location; it does not claim an independent per-file license audit. Read the [official license deed and legal-code link](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the terms accompanying your actual upstream files.

Attribution, license notices, modification notices and applicable share-alike obligations must be considered for source reuse and adaptations. This repository's software license does not override them. Other rights, including privacy or publicity interests in worker images, may require separate review. This documentation is not legal clearance for a particular redistribution or application.

Acquisition took place at the Future Factories Laboratory, University of South Carolina. Credit Harik and all coauthors through the original papers. Current collaborator affiliations do not transfer ownership or retrospectively change the acquisition site.

## This repository

The [MIT license](../LICENSE) applies to newly authored website code, catalog organization, documentation, and original illustrative scaffolding in this package. It excludes third-party source data, source articles, upstream imagery, institutional marks, and material contributed under different terms.

The demonstration is adapted from the project-owner-supplied illustrative modeling example. It contains no raw observations, real cycle identifier, or source images. The namespace `example.org` is intentionally demonstrative, not a registered MANIFOLD ontology endpoint.

## Before public release

Confirm authority to publish project branding and team descriptions; inspect license compatibility for any later additions; obtain appropriate institutional review before distributing restricted or human-associated evidence. Do not republish full source papers or generated images that imply source observations without establishing their provenance and permissions.
